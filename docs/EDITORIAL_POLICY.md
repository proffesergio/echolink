# Editorial quality and Meta publishing

Checked 2026-11-10 against the official sources below. Automated screening is
incomplete and cannot guarantee Community Standards compliance, recommendation,
copyright clearance, account eligibility or monetization.

Write a coherent, independently structured report from supported evidence. Add
useful context only when evidence supports it; do not invent expert opinion,
interviews, causes or first-hand reporting to look original. Keep allegations,
denials and uncertainty attributed. Sensitive/crisis material needs human review.
Do not disguise another outlet's prose by changing a few words. Passing the
internal copied-sentence threshold is not a legal or Meta originality test.

Use owned, licensed or otherwise permitted media. A logo, overlay, headline or
image credit alone does not establish image rights or originality. Do not present
a generated event image as a documentary photograph; apply relevant disclosure
and provenance requirements. Factual research records remain private, but material
attribution must not be removed merely to conceal how a story was sourced.

Captions must describe the actual story/design. Keep hashtags relevant and
bounded. Do not manufacture engagement, promise rewards for shares/comments, or
coordinate fake activity. Automatic self-likes are disabled on the tuned news
Page; legitimate delayed link comments remain configurable. A five-minute delay
does not guarantee reach or protection against enforcement. Respect Graph API
responses, rate-limit backoff, per-Page spacing and duplicate/receipt checks.

The platform preserves factual/crisis/originality holds, publishing receipts and
website-first verification. It does not certify the complete Community Standards
or monetization rules. Account-, country-, content- and program-specific eligibility
must be checked in Meta Professional Dashboard / Monetization / Page quality.
There is no published universal safe daily quota implemented as a Meta guarantee.
