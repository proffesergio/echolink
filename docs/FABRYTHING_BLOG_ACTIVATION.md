# Fabrything Blog — November 14, 2026

The owner authorized a separate website-only profile for `https://www.fabrything.com/blog`, including production configuration, automatic writing/review/scheduling/publication and CI/CD deployment. English launches first; Bangla is a later, separate expansion. Advertising and affiliate programme accounts are not approved yet. Commerce pages remain ad-free.

## Delivery and evidence

- `Fabrything Blog` is a website destination, not a verified Facebook Page. SQL022 adds that explicit destination and forbids Facebook identities on it. Planner, approval, task execution and UI use the same distinction.
- The initial ceiling is two guides/day, at least720 minutes apart, in an all-day America/New_York window. One preparation at a time uses the existing globally serialized, memory-protected local inference lane. The other profiles retain their settings.
- Research begins with the real Gadgets catalog and an approved pool of audio compatibility, microphone-quality, one-channel troubleshooting and upgrade-decision topics. Sources are Microsoft documentation and Bluetooth SIG documentation/reporting, fetched through the existing public-network boundary. At least two distinct usable source pages are required. New relevant Bluetooth SIG feed entries can extend the pool. Already allocated topics, including held content, are not recycled. Exhaustion pauses generation rather than manufacturing new evidence.
- The writer receives bounded source passages, catalog specifications and explicit editorial instructions. A separate source-relative model review follows deterministic factual, originality, language and crisis checks. This is not external fact verification or hands-on product testing. Insufficient or disputed evidence stays held.
- Local task routing adds `journal_article` and `journal_review`. Real-source Qwen drafts introduced unsupported compatibility claims, so the dedicated connection now routes writing and review to installed Gemma3 4B without an automatic Qwen fallback. It waits for safe memory admission rather than substituting that observed weaker route. No best-model benchmark claim is made. Existing model choices on other profiles are preserved.
- Structured articles include a descriptive slug, factual metadata, topic/intent, headings, practical prose, source attribution, store product blocks and an existing catalog image with alt text. No fabricated prices, affiliate destinations, testing claims, international delivery or ratings. Image generation/storage credentials are not required for reusing these catalog images.
- The signed bridge is workspace/profile scoped. Reviewed content hashes and exact payload identities are checked before delivery. An interrupted POST is reconciled by a signed, read-only receipt lookup; it is never blindly resubmitted. Confirmed/unknown deliveries preserve receipts and usage reservations.
- A resident-fallback admission fix avoids unloading an admitted fallback between preparation stages. Single-call serialization, reserve limits and the emergency guard remain enabled.
